Why?

The effect of a DNA edit depends on the cell type and surrounding sequence. Models that predict these effects can help select edits for a biological goal.

BEND 2.0 compares model predictions with experimental measurements. Its tasks evaluate sequence priors, functional effects, context dependence and the selection of edits for experimental testing.
